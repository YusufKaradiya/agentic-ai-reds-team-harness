# Research Questions

## RQ1

How effectively can threat-informed adversarial
testing identify prompt injection, tool abuse and
data-exfiltration risks in a tool-enabled RAG agent?

## RQ2

How does a layered defensive architecture affect
attack success rate and sensitive-data leakage
compared with an undefended baseline?

## RQ3

What trade-offs exist between security controls,
false blocking, task utility and latency?

## RQ4

How reproducible are adversarial evaluation
results across repeated executions under the
same configuration?

---

# Hypotheses

## H1

A defended agent will exhibit lower attack success
and sensitive-data leakage than the baseline agent.

## H2

Adding additional defensive controls may reduce
task utility for some legitimate requests.

## H3

Defense controls will introduce additional
processing latency.

## H4

Configuration-driven attack execution will produce
more reproducible experiments than manual testing.
